Bhavani Thekkada

She grew up on a coffee farm in Karnataka and did not see snow until she was twenty. At thirty she took a World Cup start at 66°N in northern Finland.

Reported by the Associated Press

She grew up on her family's coffee farm at Perur, near Napoklu in Kodagu, where it has never snowed and where neither of her parents had ever seen snow either. In 2013 she watched a Bollywood film with a trekking sequence in it and went looking for the mountains it was set in.

Cross-country came later, and secondhand.
