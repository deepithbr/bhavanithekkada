Partnership

Four seasons to the French Alps, and the people who get her there.

What support funds

01

Training blocks on snow

The European season opens in November against athletes who have been on snow since October. A pre-season block buys those six weeks back.

02

Competition travel

December 2025 ran Ruka to Trondheim to Davos in sixteen days. Flights, transfers and everything between the start lists are hers.

03

Coaching and wax service

Every other athlete on a World Cup start list has a team reading the snow that morning and grinding skis to it. She does not.

04

Race skis and kit

Skis are ground for the snow they will meet, so one pair cannot cover a season.

Current support

Reliance Foundation

Athlete support through their sports programme: sports psychologist, nutritionist and strength coach, and conditioning blocks in Mumbai through the Indian summer.

Government of Karnataka

State backing from her home state, the department that has recognised her results and carried her through domestic selection and the Khelo India programme.

Government of India

National backing for her international programme, recognising her results and her representation of India abroad.
