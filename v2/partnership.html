Partnership

The four seasons between here and the 2030 Games.

Where your support goes

Supporting an international cross-country skiing programme means investing in the details that make high-level competition possible.

01

Training blocks on snow

The European racing season begins in November, while many competitors have already been training on snow for weeks. Early-season training blocks are essential to arrive prepared, competitive and race-ready.

02

Competition travel

World Championships, international and national racing can mean moving between countries within days. Flights, transfers, accommodation and everything between the start lists are part of the journey.

Your support helps make international racing possible.

03

Coaching and ski preparation

At the highest level, performance extends beyond the athlete. Coaching, race preparation and professional ski preparation (including grinding, waxing and adapting skis to changing snow conditions) can make a crucial difference on race day.

04

Race skis and equipment

Cross-country skiing requires specialised equipment for different snow conditions, techniques and race formats. Your support helps provide race skis, poles, boots, race suits and the equipment needed to compete at the highest level.
