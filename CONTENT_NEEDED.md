## 1. Blocking

### 1.1 Image rights for the entire photo set

One frame extracted from `Bhavani portfolio.pdf` carries a visible **IMAGO**
picture-agency watermark. The racing cutout used in the story section appears to
come from that same frame with the watermark removed and the background cut out.

That means at least some of the race photography in the supplied portfolio is
licensed agency material, and possibly none of it is hers to publish.

Needed, per image:

- who took it
- whether she owns it or holds a licence, and what that licence permits
- the credit line to display

The watermarked frame is already excluded. Twenty-five assets are in use and all
are flagged `rights: "unconfirmed"` in `content/images.json`.

### 1.2 Take down the claims on the current site

The live site at `bt-portfolio-ddd16.web.app` names **Reliance Foundation**, the
**Government of India** and the **Karnataka Government** as partners. If no
agreement exists with each of them, that is the single largest liability in the
current build and should come down today, ahead of anything else here.

Its page title also reads "Nordic Skiing World Champion". Her best World
Championships finish is 104th at Planica. Same urgency.

## 2. Claims held back pending her confirmation

These appear in the supplied portfolio. None is published as fact. Each needs
either a source or a decision to drop it.

| Claim | Why it is held back | What would settle it |
|---|---|---|
| India's No. 1 cross-country skier | No published national ranking found. FIS publishes points, not a national number one. | The ranking authority and the list |
| First South Indian to receive recognition in skiing | Unfalsifiable as worded | Define "recognition", name the body |
| First Indian woman with an international ski instructor certificate | The 2019 New Zealand certification is credible and is published. The "first" is unsourced. | The certificate, plus whoever asserts the first |
| Committee member, ski and snowboard federation of India, appointed by IOA | No federation or IOA announcement found | The appointment letter |
| National record, 5 km, 18:10 | **18:10.4 is real** — her 5 km freestyle time at Harbin 2025, and it is published on the site as a result. Nothing confirms it as a national record. | The federation's record list |
| Only female Indian athlete at the 2025 Nordic World Ski Championships | Plausible, not checked against the Trondheim entry list | The FIS entry list |

Two need more than confirmation.

**"First Indian to reach the quarter-finals in cross-country skiing" is very
likely wrong.** She did reach the sprint quarter-finals at Harbin 2025, and that
is on the site. But Padma Namgail qualified for the quarter-finals in the men's
sprint at the same Games. Unless the claim means "first Indian woman", it should
be dropped rather than softened.

**"India's first international medal in women's cross-country skiing"** is
reported by the Associated Press and by Karnataka First, but as a characterisation
rather than a federation record. The site carries it as reported speech, attributed.
Ask whether she wants it stated more strongly, and if so, who will stand behind it.

## 3. Content that would improve the site

1. Preferred public name. The site uses "Bhavani Thekkada" as the wordmark and
   "Bhavani Thekkada Nanjunda" in full. Confirm which she prefers.
2. One line of professional positioning, in her words.
3. An approved biography she is happy to have quoted from.
4. Her competitive goal now that Milano Cortina has passed. The site deliberately

### The record

6. The full verified medal list. The domestic rows on the site come from the
   portfolio and are labelled "to confirm".
7. Khelo India 2025 is contradictory in the sources. The portfolio says gold in the
   5 km and silver in the 1.4 km sprint. Press reporting on KIWG 2026 refers to a

   location. The photograph on file appears to be from Almaty.

### Partnership

11. The current sponsorship requirement. The €20,400 figure in
    `bhavanithekkada 2026.pdf` is historical and is deliberately absent from the site.
12. Current training base, coach and support team.
13. Equipment partners. The photographs show Fischer skis and Leki poles; whether
    those are supported or bought is not something the site should assume.

### Assets

15. Race photography she owns.
16. Anything at all from Kodagu. This is the biggest gap. The site's whole argument

    outside Vidhana Soudha in Bengaluru, which is doing a lot of work.
17. Mountaineering photographs, particularly from Elbrus.
18. Video, if any exists and is hers.
19. Certificates and awards, as scans.
20. Her current press kit.

### Media

21. Press links she wants featured. The site currently lists four.
22. Any broadcast or podcast appearances.

### Boundaries

23. **Anything she does not want public.** Her parents are named in the portfolio
    and in regional press. The site does not name them, on the basis that family
    detail should be opt-in. Same question for her date of birth, which is on her
    FIS profile and so is already public, and her phone number.
