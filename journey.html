Kodagu to France

A journey across continents, seasons, and start lines.

Scroll through the years that shaped her journey

  1. 1995 · Kodagu, Karnataka

    Perur, near Napoklu

    Born 14 May 1995 in the coffee-growing hills of Kodagu. It rains hard there for four months a year and it has never snowed. The nearest snow sits two thousand kilometres north and four thousand metres up.

  2. 2013

    Joined the NCC

    The National Cadet Corps, two years before the All India camp and three before the Republic Day contingent.

  3. 2014 · Manali and Darjeeling

    The mountains came first

    There was no ski club to come up through, so the mountains did that job. A Grade A adventure course at ABVIMAS, then Special Basic Mountaineering at the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute in Darjeeling.

  4. 2015 · India

    Through the NCC

    The camp that put her in front of the selectors for the Republic Day contingent the following January.

  5. 2016 · Rajpath, New Delhi

    The Republic Day Parade

    Selected for the NCC contingent at the Republic Day Parade, a year after the All India camp.

  6. 2017 · Himalayan Mountaineering Institute, Darjeeling

    Instructing at HMI

    Method of Instruction at HMI Darjeeling, then a year there as a guest instructor. Three years after her first course she was teaching the same ground.

  7. 2018 · Kashmir and the Caucasus

    Elbrus, and the start of skiing

    Basic and intermediate skiing at the Jawaharlal Institute of Mountaineering, advanced skiing at the Indian Institute of Skiing and Mountaineering in Kashmir. The same year she climbed Mount Elbrus, at 5,642 m the highest summit in Europe.

  8. 2019 · Mt. Ruapehu, New Zealand

    Ruapehu

    An international snowsports instructor certification taken on an active volcano with a ski field on it, and the first qualification she earned outside India. A season skiing abroad followed.

  9. 2020 · Gulmarg, Jammu and Kashmir

    The switch

    Cross-country replaced alpine. Two bronzes at the Khelo India Winter Games that season, over 5 km and 10 km.

  10. 2021 · Gulmarg, Jammu and Kashmir

    Khelo India gold

    Gold over 1.4 km and bronze over 5 km at the Khelo India Winter Games, in her second season on cross-country skis.

  11. 2022 · Akureyri, Iceland

    Akureyri

    Her first internationally scored starts, in the sprint and the 10 km interval start, in January. Seventh in sprint qualification and sixth over the distance. At home the same season: golds and silvers across the national cross-country and biathlon championships.

  12. 2023 · Planica, Slovenia

    Nordic World Ski Championships

    A first Worlds, in the 10 km interval start. Earlier that season she returned to Akureyri and finished fourth in both the sprint and the interval start.

  13. 2024 · Schuchinsk, Idre, Seefeld, Lygna

    Kazakhstan, then Scandinavia

    Continental cup racing at Schuchinsk in March, then three FIS meetings across Sweden, Austria and Norway in December: the first sustained block of racing on European snow. Three golds at the Khelo India Winter Games at home.

  14. 2025 · Harbin, Trondheim, Corralco, Ruka

    The season it turned

    India's flag bearer at the Asian Winter Games opening ceremony in Harbin, and a sprint quarter-finalist there. Two bronzes on consecutive days at Corralco in Chile, reported in India as the first international cross-country medals won by an Indian woman. Then a first World Cup start at Ruka, forty kilometres short of the Arctic Circle.

  15. 2026 · Gulmarg, Jammu and Kashmir

    The Worldloppet circuit

    Her first season on the Worldloppet marathon circuit. At home the same season: gold in the 1.5 km sprint at the Khelo India Winter Games in 4:13.91, with bronzes over 10 km and 15 km.

  16. 2027 · Falun, Sweden

    World Championships, Falun

    A third start at the Worlds, with two editions already behind her.

  17. 2028

    Points, and the calendar

    Race starts and FIS points under the qualification rules, the season that sets up everything after it.

  18. 2029 · Almaty and Lahti

    Almaty, then Lahti

    Almaty, then Lahti, and the points that decide 2030.

  19. 2030 · La Clusaz, Haute-Savoie

    Winter Olympics

    The reason the rest of it is planned the way it is. Cross-country at the 2030 Games is held at La Clusaz, on the Confins plateau, 1 to 17 February.
