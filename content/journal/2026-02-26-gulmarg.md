The sprint went first. One and a half kilometres, run as heats, and the fastest
qualifier does not always win because you have to do it four times in an
afternoon. Four minutes and thirteen seconds in the final.

Then the distance races. Ten kilometres and fifteen kilometres, two days, alone
against the clock each time. Bronze in both.

Three medals for Karnataka at a Games held in Kashmir, which is roughly three
thousand kilometres from where I learned to walk.
