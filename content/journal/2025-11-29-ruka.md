Ruka sits at 66 degrees north, a short drive south of the Arctic Circle. Kodagu,
where I was born, sits at 12 degrees north and has never had snow. That is the
whole distance, in two numbers.

Two races in two days. The ten kilometre interval start on the Friday, the
sprint on the Saturday. Eighty-first and seventy-sixth.

Those are not numbers anyone celebrates. They are also a World Cup start list
with my name on it, which is not a thing that has happened often for an Indian
woman in this sport, and it is the list I intend to keep appearing on.
