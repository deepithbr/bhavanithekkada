Speaking

The storyteller is the reason she is here.

What she brings Six things a decade on snow is worth to a room.

Resilience

Most of the work happens before anyone is watching. She reached a World Cup start line from a place with no snow, and can show a room how to keep moving when nothing yet confirms it is working.

Corporates and leadership programmes

Endurance

Endurance is a plan, not a feeling. A 10 km race is mapped to the heartbeat months before it is run, and she takes an audience inside how that hour is built and what it actually costs.

Sports organisations and teams

Representation

Being first is not the same as being ready. She carried India’s flag into an Asian Winter Games as the only one in her discipline, and speaks to what that asks of a person and what it opens for the next.

Women's events and panels

Apprenticeship

Talent takes more credit than it earns. Before the racing came glaciers, rope work and instructing jobs, and she makes the case for the unglamorous years that build somebody capable of the good ones.

Schools and universities

Persuasion

Nobody funds what they cannot picture. Almost every season was paid for by explaining a sport the listener had never seen, and she can show how to make an unfamiliar idea land with the person who can back it.

Conferences and fireside conversations

Pathways

A route only exists once somebody has walked it. She built one out of India in a sport that had none, and works on what it takes to make a path clear enough for the next person to follow.

Federations and sports bodies
